Features

Compose scenes like a sketchbook

Layer-based scenes

Every scene is a stack. Add, reorder, hide, and lock layers for sets, characters, props, and cameras — independently.

Timeline editing

Sequence your shots on an iMovie-style timeline. Scrub, trim, and preview without leaving the composer.

Live 3D viewport

See your stacked layers in real 3D space. Orbit, frame shots, and check depth as you build.

Made for crews

Designed around the Playtime pipeline — a shared vocabulary of scenes, shots, and layers your whole team gets instantly.

Fast & local

An Electron front-end with a Python engine underneath. Everything runs on your machine — no cloud, no waiting.

Built to grow

v0.0.1 is just the base layer. New tools land with every release as the film moves through production.

Workflow

Three steps to a shot

01 · STACK

Build your layers

Drop in your set, characters, and props as separate layers, just like pages in a sketchbook.

02 · COMPOSE

Frame it in 3D

Position everything in the viewport, set your camera layer, and dial in the shot.

03 · CUT

Edit on the timeline

Sequence shots into scenes, scrub the preview, and hand off to the rest of the crew.
